Reading

A beta of 0.37 means that when AOK moves by 1%, the fund moves on average by 0.37%: the fund is less sensitive to its market than the benchmark. The annualised alpha of 4.85% is the return earned on top of what this level of market risk would explain. With an R² of 0.28, 28% of the fund's daily variance is explained by the benchmark. The rolling beta shows how stable this exposure has been.
