Our approach

We manage $1bn in each fund. Serenity protects capital with bonds, defensive stocks and cash-like ETFs. Velocity targets growth with at least 70% in equities. Each universe holds 25 assets, picked class by class (stocks, ETFs, bonds, crypto, commodities) with z-scores on momentum, volatility and beta, filtered for correlation, then weighted by a constrained Sharpe optimiser on three years of Yahoo Finance data.
