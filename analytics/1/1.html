Serenity Fund - asset selection and z-scores

Investment universe

The Serenity Fund (capital preservation for risk-averse clients) selects 25 assets, split into 4 groups. All data come from Yahoo Finance over the last three years (2023-11-20 to 2026-11-17).

  • Bonds (8): SGOV, BIL, VGSH, SJNK, STIP, BNDX, VCIT, SCHP
  • Defensive equities (10): KO, AEP, CB, BRK-B, DUK, SO, LIN, ED, WEC, XEL
  • Low-volatility equity ETFs (5): QUAL, EFAV, DGRO, XLP, ACWV
  • Commodities & real estate (2): DBC, VNQ

Method

Each group is scored on its own indicators, so that a bond ETF is only compared with other bond ETFs. For every indicator we compute the z-score inside the group, z = (x − mean) / standard deviation, and orient it so that a positive value is favourable for this fund. The score is the average of the oriented z-scores. In the table, each cell shows the raw value and its oriented z-score in brackets.
