Velocity Fund - asset selection and z-scores

Investment universe

The Velocity Fund (long-term growth for clients who accept higher risk) selects 25 assets, split into 4 groups. All data come from Yahoo Finance over the last three years (2023-11-20 to 2026-11-17).

  • Equities (13): JNJ, TRGP, KO, CB, MU, ROST, ED, XOM, AMGN, CVX, OKE, MRK, COP
  • Equity ETFs (7): EFAV, SMH, XLV, QUAL, XLP, EWZ, SCHD
  • Bonds (2): VTIP, SJNK
  • Commodities, real estate & crypto (3): PDBC, VNQ, BTC-USD

Method

Each group is scored on its own indicators, so that a bond ETF is only compared with other bond ETFs. For every indicator we compute the z-score inside the group, z = (x − mean) / standard deviation, and orient it so that a positive value is favourable for this fund. The score is the average of the oriented z-scores. In the table, each cell shows the raw value and its oriented z-score in brackets.
