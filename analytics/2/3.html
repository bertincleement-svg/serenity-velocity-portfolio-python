Velocity Fund - daily returns

On the graph

The first chart shows the daily returns of every asset; the box plots compare their distributions. The most volatile asset is MU (66.7% annualised) and the least volatile is VTIP (1.9%). The worst single day in the universe was -19.82% for EWZ.

The fund's own daily returns have a skewness of -0.62 and an excess kurtosis of 10.47: the tails are fatter than a normal distribution, which is why the VaR is computed historically rather than only with a normal model.

Correlation between assets

The correlation matrix shows how the 25 assets move together (+1: identical moves, 0: unrelated, -1: opposite moves). The average pairwise correlation is 0.22: the lower it is, the more diversified the fund. The most correlated pair is XOM / CVX (0.83) and the least correlated pair is ED / SMH (-0.38). This is the effect of the correlation filter applied when the assets were selected.
