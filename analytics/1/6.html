Serenity Fund - 10-day 99% VaR

Method

We apply the fund's weights to three years of daily returns and compound them over every overlapping 10-day window (741 windows). The 10-day 99% historical VaR is the loss exceeded in only 1% of these windows, applied to $1bn. The expected shortfall is the average loss beyond the VaR.

Reading

Over the last three years, the Serenity Fund would have lost more than $15.4m over 10 trading days in only 1% of cases, i.e. 1.54% of its capital. The historical VaR is close to or below the normal VaR over this period.
