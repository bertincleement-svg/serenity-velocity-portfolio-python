Serenity Fund - daily returns

On the graph

The first chart shows the daily returns of every asset; the box plots compare their distributions. The most volatile asset is XEL (20.5% annualised) and the least volatile is SGOV (0.2%). The worst single day in the universe was -8.64% for XEL.

The fund's own daily returns have a skewness of -0.39 and an excess kurtosis of 11.47: the tails are fatter than a normal distribution, which is why the VaR is computed historically rather than only with a normal model.

Correlation between assets

The correlation matrix shows how the 25 assets move together (+1: identical moves, 0: unrelated, -1: opposite moves). The average pairwise correlation is 0.28: the lower it is, the more diversified the fund. The most correlated pair is VCIT / SCHP (0.85) and the least correlated pair is BNDX / DBC (-0.31). This is the effect of the correlation filter applied when the assets were selected.
